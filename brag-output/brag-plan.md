# Brag Plan: Triostepdkhyber

## What is this app?
Triostepdkhyber is an artisanal footwear house crafting luxury Chelsea boots from full-grain Italian leather with Goodyear-welted construction.

## The angle
Quiet luxury meets architectural craft. Instead of generic e-commerce promotions, the video is an uncompromising, editorial showcase of heritage craftsmanship, timeless silhouettes, and enduring leather quality built to last decades.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-contrast obsidian black screen revealing the massive typographic identity "TRIOSTEPDKHYBER" and the resolute claim: "THE NEW STANDARD OF HERITAGE FOOTWEAR."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The signature Heritage Collection lineup: Classic Black Leather (24,900 Rs), Desert Suede Boot (22,500 Rs), Chunky Sole Platform (28,000 Rs).
- The Crafting Pillars: Tuscan full-grain hides, Goodyear-welted resolable construction, and millimeter precision.

## Outro / punchline
The sharp, elegant call to action: "ENTER THE SHOP" — luxury that grows richer with every step.

## User flow worth showing
The Heritage Showcase flow: Entry Statement (Editorial Hero) → The Collection (Signature Boot Selection & Prices) → The Craft (Tuscan leather & Goodyear welt) → The Destination (Enter The Shop).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet luxury editorial product film
- Interpretation: Confident restraint, slow deliberate reveals, generous typography letter-spacing, and warm subtle audio cues.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #1a1a1a (Dark obsidian) & #f8f8f8 (Clean studio light)
- Accent: #d4af37 (Warm gold / brass) & #ffffff (Pure white)
- Text: #ffffff (on dark) & #1a1a1a (on light)
- Display font: Oswald (Google Fonts, uppercase, tracked)
- Body font: Inter (Google Fonts, clean geometric sans)
- Strongest visual element: Stark typography hierarchy, high-fashion editorial product photography, process badges.

## Share copy (draft)
Introducing Triostepdkhyber: The new standard of heritage footwear. Handcrafted from full-grain Italian leather, Goodyear-welted, and built to last decades.

## Audio direction
- Role: steady and clean professional bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: Starts at 0.0s at volume 0.32, steady rhythm, soft fade-out across final 2.0s
- Music cue guidance: Preset available (109.96 BPM). Key cues at 5.34s (Scene 1 to 2 transition), 10.93s (Scene 2 to 3 transition), 15.84s (Scene 3 to 4 transition).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; background warmth and frame presence breathe slightly with music RMS.
- SFX posture: minimal and restrained (2-3 subtle cues)
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - 0.5s: Soft drop / low impact for brand mark entrance (`interface/drop_001.ogg`)
  - 5.34s: Smooth card slide for boot collection reveal (`casino/card-slide-1.ogg`)
  - 15.84s: Subtle confirmation chime on final CTA (`interface/bong_001.ogg`)
- Restraint rule: No harsh buzzers, no aggressive glitches, no distracting volume spikes.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Standard (Hook) — 5.0s
Obsidian dark background (#1a1a1a). Subtle geometric border framing. Large uppercase display typography "TRIOSTEPDKHYBER" fades up with subtle tracking expansion. Below it, the manifesto: "THE NEW STANDARD OF HERITAGE FOOTWEAR" settles. A subtle badge reads "EST. 2026 // HANDCRAFTED LUXURY".
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: Set an aura of prestige and uncompromising craft.
Audio-coupled idea: Soft drop sound at 0.5s as brand mark locks in.
Music: Volume 0.32 steady intro.
Transition mood: Soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Heritage Collection — 5.5s
Switch to clean, illuminated studio background (#f8f8f8). Header "THE HERITAGE COLLECTION" anchors the top. 3 distinct product cards slide in:
1. Classic Black Leather — 24,900 Rs
2. Desert Suede Boot — 22,500 Rs
3. Chunky Sole Platform — 28,000 Rs
Each card features actual high-definition boot photography from the project with clean typography labels.
Sequential/interaction: 3 boot cards reveal consecutively across beats.
Audio intent: Elevate the tangible product value and design purity.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle card slide sound on the initial product cascade.
Transition mood: Soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Uncompromising Craft — 5.0s
Back to dark obsidian palette (#1a1a1a). Headline "CRAFTING LEGACY" with an elegant gold divider line. 3 craftsmanship pillars appear:
- "Tuscan Full-Grain Leather" (Patina that grows richer with every step)
- "Goodyear-Welted Construction" (Built to last decades, resoled indefinitely)
- "Millimeter Precision" (Architectural design meets master shoemaking)
Sequential/interaction: 3 pillars fade up in balanced rhythm.
Audio intent: Convey timeless durability, premium materials, and authentic heritage.
Audio-coupled idea: Beat-aligned typographic focus.
Transition mood: Soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro / Enter The Shop — 4.5s
Dark minimalist backdrop. Centered luxury lockup: "TRIOSTEPDKHYBER". CTA button: "ENTER THE SHOP". Subtext: "triostepdkhyber.com".
Sequential/interaction: CTA button gently scales in with subtle border glow.
Audio intent: Confident closure and luxury brand permanence.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle chime / tone at 16.0s.
Music: Gentle fade to silence over the last 1.5s.

**Music mood for this video:** Steady and clean luxury bed
**Audio summary:** Restrained, elegant beat providing rhythmic motion beneath high-contrast editorial visuals, resolving in a gentle fade.
